# Portfolio copy benchmark

Research and revision: 2026-09-23. Scope: copy only; existing design retained.

## Method and limits

Reviewed current first-party pages and compared their messaging with Nicholas Carter's supplied Full Stack and Data & AI resumes. This is a qualitative editorial analysis, not a conversion study or a ranking of engineers. The sample includes Google/Meta alumni and other established engineers; not every subject is a FAANG or staff engineer. Several sites serve publication or teaching businesses, so their calls to action are not direct models for a job search.

The configured search service was unavailable, and public search pages blocked automated discovery. Sources below were read directly. No claims about competitor hiring success, traffic, or conversion were inferred. No competitor copy was reused.

## Findings on the previous copy

1. “Built for people. Made to last.” did not identify an engineer or a specific contribution.
2. The long career paragraph repeated information in the experience entries.
3. The homepage named employers but hid the strongest client/product evidence in resume bullets.
4. Leadership principles were plausible but interchangeable with almost any senior engineer's portfolio.
5. The main contact action was at the bottom. Both hero links only offered further browsing.
6. Recent projects were technically precise but sometimes led with architecture instead of the problem.
7. “Try routing” pointed to a repository rather than a direct demo destination.

## Changes applied

- Explicit senior-engineer headline; named AgileThought clients in the introduction.
- Direct “Discuss a role” email action alongside the resume link.
- Career summary shortened to 67 words; six-role chronology retained on resume routes.
- Homepage experience now names Deloitte Symphony, Bankers Surety, VF Corp brands, Sysco, and Member Portal R2 with correct employer/client attribution.
- Principles anchored to actual consulting, Bankers Surety delivery, and Mad Mobile mentoring work.
- Project descriptions explain a problem and design choice; fixture, alpha, and incomplete-enforcement boundaries remain visible.
- Verdict demo description now says it is included in the repository.
- Clear hiring/project contact prompt instead of a generic closing slogan.
- No changes to dates, employer titles, metrics, availability, email, or CSS.

## Evidence boundaries and follow-up opportunities

The supplied resumes and owner-confirmed 20+ years support the career facts. They do not establish quantified revenue, user counts, performance improvements, team sizes, or current staff employment; none were added. Names of clients remain attributed to their actual consulting employer.

Future case studies could become stronger with owner-approved details about a difficult technical decision, delivery constraints, team scope, or measured result. These are optional content opportunities, not launch blockers. Do not add a blog, testimonials, client logos, or invented metrics merely because a comparator uses them.
